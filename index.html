Zwap

Currency and unit conversion, and a world clock, for iPhone.

Two cards, one number, and a Z to swap between them. Money uses the European Central Bank's daily rates; Units converts across 13 categories on your phone, online or not; World Clock shows the time in any zone on a globe you can spin.
